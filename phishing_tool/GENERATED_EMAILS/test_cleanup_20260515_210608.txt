EDUCATIONAL TEMPLATE: Email Spoofing
--------------------------------------------------
From: CEO <ceo@company.com>
Subject: Urgent: Wire transfer required

Example:
From: Jane Doe <jane.doe@company.com>
To: finance@company.com
Subject: Urgent: approve wire transfer

A malicious actor forges the From header so the email appears to come from a trusted executive.
Why it works: Attackers spoof display names and use subtle domain typos to exploit trust.
Defense: Verify sender email addresses, inspect full headers, and call known contacts on their published numbers.
