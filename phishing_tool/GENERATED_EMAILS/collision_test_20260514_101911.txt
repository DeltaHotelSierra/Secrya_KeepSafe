EDUCATIONAL TEMPLATE: Vishing (Voice Phishing)
--------------------------------------------------
Example voicemail/call script:
  Caller: Bank Security
  Message: We need to confirm recent transactions; please call back and verify your card number.

Explanation: Attackers use phone calls to impersonate trusted organizations and extract sensitive information.
Defense: Do not provide sensitive data over unsolicited calls; hang up and call the official number from your account statement.
